**2:285**

آمَنَ الرَّسُولُ بِمَا أُنْزِلَ إِلَيْهِ مِنْ رَبِّهِ وَالْمُؤْمِنُونَ ۚ كُلُّ آمَنَ بِاللَّهِ وَمَلَائِكَتِهِ وَكُتُبِهِ وَرُسُلِهِ لَا نُفَرِّقُ بَيْنَ أَحَدٍ مِنْ رُسُلِهِ ۚ وَقَالُوا سَمِعْنَا وَأَطَعْنَا ۖ غُفْرَانَكَ رَبَّنَا وَإِلَيْكَ الْمَصِيرُ

## Transliteration

**2:285**  
*Aamana ar-rasoolu bimaa unzila ilayhi min rabbihi wal-mu’minoon. Kullun aamana billahi wa malaa’ikatahu wa kutubihi wa rusulihi. Laa nufarriqu baina ahadin min rusulihi; wa qaaloo sami’na wa ata’na. Ghufranaka rabbanaa wa ilayka al-maseer.*

**2:286**  
*Laa yukallifu Allahu nafsan illa wus’aha; lahaa ma kasabat wa ‘alayha ma iktasabat. Rabbanaa la tu’akhidhnaa in naseenaa aw akhta’naa; rabbanaa wa la tuhammilnaa isran kama hamaltahu ‘ala alladheena min qablinaa; rabbanaa wa la tuhammilnaa ma la taaqata lanaa bihi; wa’fu ‘annaa waghfir lanaa warhamnaa; anta mawlanaa fansurna ‘alal qawmi l-kaafireen.*

## English Translation (Sahih International)

**2:285**  
> The Messenger (ﷺ) believes in what has been revealed to him from his Lord, and [so do] the believers. Each one believes in Allah, His angels, His Books, and His messengers. *“We make no distinction between any of His messengers.”* And they say, *“We hear and we obey. [We seek] Your forgiveness, our Lord! And to You is the final return.”*

**2:286**  
> *Allah does not charge a soul except [with that within] its capacity.* It will have [the consequence of] what [good] it has gained, and it will bear [the consequence of] what [evil] it has earned. *“Our Lord! Do not impose blame upon us if we forget or make a mistake. Our Lord! Do not lay upon us a burden like that which You laid upon those before us. Our Lord! Do not burden us with that which we have no ability to bear. Pardon us; and forgive us; and have mercy upon us. You are our protector, so give us victory over the disbelieving people.”*

## Urdu Translation

رسول (صلىٰ اللہ علیہ وسلم) نے اس چیز پر ایمان لایا جو اس کے رب کی طرف سے نازل ہوئی اور مؤمنین بھی۔ یہ سب اللہ پر، اس کے فرشتوں پر، اس کی کتابوں پر اور اس کے رسولوں پر ایمان لائے۔ *(ہم کسی بھی رسول میں فرق نہیں کرتے)* وہ کہتے ہیں، “ہم نے سنا اور اطاعت کی؛ اے ہمارے رب! ہم تیری بخشش مانگتے ہیں اور تیری ہی طرف لوٹ کر جانا ہے۔”

**2:286 (اردو)**  
اللہ کسی جان پر اس کی طاقت کے سوا بوجھ نہیں ڈالتا۔ جو نیکی کسی نے کمایی اس کا اجر اسی کے لیے ہے اور جو برائی اس نے کمایی اس کا بوجھ اسی پر ہے۔ *(اے ہمارے رب! اگر ہم بھول جائیں یا خطا کر بیٹھیں تو ہمیں نہ پکڑنا۔ اے ہمارے رب! ہم پر ایسا بوجھ نہ ڈال جیسا تو نے پہلے لوگوں پر ڈالا تھا۔ اے ہمارے رب! اور ہم پر ایسا بوجھ نہ ڈال جسے ہم اٹھا نہ سکیں۔ ہمیں معاف کر، ہمیں بخش دے اور ہم پر رحم فرما۔ تو ہمارا مالک ہے، ہمیں کافروں پر فتح دے۔)*

## Why These Verses Matter

- **They summarize core faith:** Verse 285 affirms belief in Allah, angels, books, and messengers.  
- **They end with a heartfelt dua:** Verse 286 asks mercy, forgiveness, and ease.  
- **Spiritual protection:** The Prophet ﷺ taught that reciting the last two verses at night is sufficient protection for that night *(Hadith: Ibn Majah 1369, Abu Dawud 1397)*.

---

## Tafsir Highlights

- **“Allah burdens no soul beyond its capacity”** — Comfort for believers that Allah judges fairly and never beyond ability.  
- **Comprehensive dua:** The prayer covers every need — forgiveness, ease, mercy, and victory.  
- **Tafsir Ibn Kathir** notes that these verses are among the greatest sources of comfort in Islam.

---

## Practical Applications

- **Night routine:** Recite both verses before sleep for spiritual safety.  
- **In hardship:** Read verse 286 when feeling overwhelmed; it reminds you Allah never gives more than you can bear.  
- **Teaching children:** Use transliteration to help them memorize step by step.

##  FAQs

**Q: What are the last two ayat of Surah Baqarah?**  
A: They are verses **2:285** and **2:286**, ending the longest surah in the Qur’an. They summarize faith and conclude with a dua.

**Q: What does “Allah does not burden a soul beyond its capacity” mean?**  
A: It means Allah is just and merciful — He never tests beyond what a person can bear.

**Q: Why should we recite the last two verses at night?**  
A: The Prophet ﷺ said whoever recites them at night will be protected — they will suffice him for that night.

**Q: How can I memorize these verses easily?**  
A: Recite one line daily using transliteration, then listen to a slow recitation to match pronunciation.
